# Remote work and home-office policy

Employees who work remotely for two or more days each week may request a
monthly home-office allowance. The allowance covers ordinary utility and
internet costs; it is not a reimbursement for furniture or computer hardware.

Submit the request in Workday before the tenth day of the month. A manager must
approve the arrangement before Finance releases the allowance. Remote workers
must use the company VPN for internal systems and must not print confidential
material at a shared workspace.
